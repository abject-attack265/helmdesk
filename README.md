Once you install the program, the main window will open. Follow these steps to configure your account.

1. Launch Helmdesk from your desktop shortcut.
2. Create an administrator account using your work email address.
3. Choose a strong password to protect your support data.
4. Connect your company email accounts to the dashboard.
5. The system will sync your messages automatically.

## 💡 Using AI Tools

Helmdesk uses internal AI models to help your team. You do not need to train these models. The system works as soon as you connect your inbox.

* Draft Replies: Click the AI button when you open a customer message. The software generates a response based on your past conversations.
* Categorize Tickets: The software marks messages by topic, such as billing, technical support, or feedback.
* Search History: Type keywords into the search bar to find old conversations. The AI understands the meaning of your search terms even if you do not use exact words.

## 🛡 Keeping Data Private

You own your data. Because you run Helmdesk on your own hardware, your customers' messages never leave your control. The AI processes information locally on your machine. This approach protects sensitive data from third-party access.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

If the software does not work as expected, check these common items.

* Check your internet connection: Some AI features require a link to external servers to fetch updates.
* Restart the app: Close Helmdesk and open it again to refresh the background services.
* Check for updates: We release improvements regularly. Visit the download link again if the software performs slowly or shows errors.
* Free up memory: Close other heavy programs if your computer runs slowly while using Helmdesk.
